## Is it dangerous? — short answer: no

- **FCDO (updated 20 Aug 2026, current 20 Sep):** advises against travel only in Cabo Delgado (except Pemba/Palma/Afungi), Memba/Eráti (Nampula) and Mecula/Marrupa (Niassa). **Inhambane province, Vilanculos and the archipelago carry no restriction.** Pemba is ~1,300 km north; the insurgency has never reached this coast.
- Vilanculos is an established tourist town; crime against visitors is rare and petty (phones, cash). Don't walk the town beach after dark, don't drive the N1 at night. On the islands the risk is essentially nil.
- **Health is the real "danger": malaria risk is HIGH year-round** (NaTHNaC), unlike Botswana in September. Take **atovaquone/proguanil (Malarone)**: start the day before, daily, and 7 days after. Buy in Cape Town Tue/Wed. Repellent at dusk, sleep under the net. Also a 2026 cholera outbreak in four provinces (not Inhambane as listed) — bottled water, lodge food only. Dengue present.
- Sea: strong tidal currents in the channels; snorkel and dive with the lodge, not solo. Cyclone season is Nov–Apr, so not a factor.
- Medical evacuation insurance is included in the &Beyond and Azura rates; if you stay on the mainland, check your own policy covers Mozambique.
