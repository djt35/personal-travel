  • ~16:30 arrive Areitio Etxea (check-in from 15:00) — unpack, let Noah loose in the garden/pool.

👦 Noah: flight + drive is a big day — keep it gentle. Grab groceries (Eroski, Durango) for relaxed pool breakfasts.
Day 2 · Thu 18 JunSan Sebastián + the flysch coast
🚗 Areitio → San Sebastián ~40 min, short hops after
Morning
  • Slow start by the pool, then drive to San Sebastián.
  • Walk La Concha; ride the Monte Igueldo funicular to the cliff-top vintage fairground — gentle rides for Noah, knockout bay views.
Afternoon
  • Aquarium of San Sebastián (shark tunnel, touch pools) — also the rainy-day backup.
  • ~30 min west to Zumaia: the flysch cliffs & San Telmo hermitage (GoT Dragonstone) at Itzurun beach.
Evening
  • Pintxos crawl in the Parte Vieja ~19:30 (early = kid-friendly): Gandarías, La Cuchara de San Telmo, Borda Berri.
🍷 ~40-min drive home → one of you stays off the txakoli, or make it an early dinner and head back.
Day 3 · Fri 19 JunGaztelugatxe + Urdaibai biosphere
🚗 Areitio → Gaztelugatxe ~50 min, loop back
Morning
  • Pool, then drive NW to San Juan de Gaztelugatxe — the 241-step causeway to the island chapel. Go early. (book free tickets ↗)
Afternoon
  • 10 min to Mundaka — famous left-hand surf wave + estuary lunch.
  • Then Laida/Laga beaches (wide Urdaibai sands) or Gernika (Tree of Gernika, Peace Museum).
Evening
  • Back to Areitio for a pool evening + BBQ.
🎟️ Gaztelugatxe needs a free timed ticket booked in advance at tickets.bizkaia.eus. No tickets? The car-park-side viewpoint is stunning on its own — or swap in Pagoeta natural park (waterfalls, forest, farm animals).
Day 4 · Sat 20 JunDrive west into Cantabria
🚗 Areitio → Cantabria base · ~210 km (2h30) — the big transfer day
⏰ Checkout before 10:00 at Areitio — pack Friday night, roll by ~09:45.
Pick ONE major stop
  • A · Animals: drive through → Cabárceno Wildlife Park (open-range elephants, bears, big cats + cable car; 3–4 hrs — Noah highlight).
  • B · Storybook + caves: Santillana del Mar (one of Spain's prettiest villages) + Museo de Altamira cave-art replica.
  • C · Culture en route: a proper Bilbao Guggenheim visit (35 min from Areitio; Puppy, Maman) then a lighter arrival.
Evening
  • Check in to Santemar on El Sardinero beach (Santander) — beach stroll, relaxed dinner, early-ish night.
🏖️ Great weather backup: a Cantabrian beach — Somo/Loredo or Suances.

Good to know

🧳 Packing

  • Layers + light rain jacket — Atlantic sirimiri drizzle even in June.
  • Swimwear & towels (beaches + the pool).
  • Comfy shoes — Gaztelugatxe steps & Santillana cobbles.
  • Sun cream, a small daypack.

🚗 Driving

  • A-8/AP-8 has tolls — a contactless card works at booths.
  • Drive on the right; roads excellent.
  • Lanes up to Areitio are narrow but easy.
  • Everything from base is a 30–50 min drive — plan a driver for pintxos nights.
