Before you go — do these first

①Apply for 3× UK ETA at gov.uk/eta — £20 each (£60 total), Noah included. Allow up to 3 working days. This is the one thing you must do before the tunnel.
②Book LeShuttle — Tue 14 out / Mon 20 back. Midweek slots are cheapest (~£150–280 return booked ahead).
③Book hotels fast — it's last-minute peak summer. Pick one from each base below; the two iconic Whitstable seafront hotels are sold out online (call direct, see note).
④Charge plan — top up at the Folkestone terminal Supercharger on arrival; Maidstone (M20 J7) & Ashford are your Tesla anchors.

Where to stay — pick one per base

Two bases, 3 nights each. Prices are approx 3-night family-room totals (GBP) — confirm live at booking. Ratings are Booking-style /10. No boutique seafront hotel on this coast has a pool — only W3 Thanington does; otherwise lean on the sandy beaches (or a Bannatyne Broadstairs pool day-pass).
