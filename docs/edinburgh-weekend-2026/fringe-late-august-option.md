### Notes on the neighbourhoods you asked about
- **Stockbridge** — only real option is **The Raeburn**, and it's excellent (9.3) but priced as a treat. Lovely for a couple; 20-min walk in.

### Avoid
Student halls (Destiny/Beaverbank/Argyl) are cheap ($265–375) but score 7.1–7.7 and are dorm-block stock — fine for a solo Fringe performer, wrong for a couple's weekend. Britannia Edinburgh scores **5.9** across 9.6k reviews; easyHotel **5.8**. Cheap for a reason.

## 🎭 Recommendation

**Book Apex City of Edinburgh** (~€610 + levy) — 0.1 mi from the centre, 8.7 across 3,566 reviews, cheapest good central option. At Fringe the thing that matters most is falling out of a late show and being 5 minutes from bed. Old Town location beats every neighbourhood play here.

**If you want it to feel like a proper couple's weekend rather than a city break**, take **The Raeburn** in Stockbridge (~€1,040, **free cancellation**, 9.3). The extra ~€430 buys a genuinely lovely hotel and quiet mornings, at the cost of a 20-min walk each way. Free cancellation means you can hold it and decide later.

**Either way: book the hotel today.** Flights at €664 are stable and there's only one direct — the hotel is the scarce thing, and 80 properties in a 95%-occupancy city goes fast.
