La Herradura sits in a sheltered horseshoe bay about 1 h (75 km, A-7) east of Málaga airport, 20 min east of Nerja. Cliffs (Cerro Gordo, Punta de la Mona) shelter it from wind, so it is a degree or so warmer than Málaga.

## What to do there with Noah
- **Playa de La Herradura** (2 km bay, calm, beach bars) and **Marina del Este** (tiny cove, snorkelling, kayaks and paddle-boards).
- **Cerro Gordo / Punta de la Mona** viewpoints, short drives above the bay.
- **Almuñécar:** Peñón del Santo lookout, the Majuelo botanical park with the Roman fish-salting tanks, the Aquatropic water park (check if open in November) and the Loro Sexi bird park.
- **Nerja caves** (20 min) and **Frigiliana** white village (30 min): good for the 4–5 Nov showers.
- **Granada / Alhambra** is 1 h inland, but too long a day for a 5-year-old in showery weather unless it is a must.
