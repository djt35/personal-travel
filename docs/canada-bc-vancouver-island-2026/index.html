🍁 West Coast Canada · BC + Vancouver Island

Fri 10 – Sat 18 July 2026 · 8 nts · family of 3 · Vancouver → Victoria → Tofino loop

A Vancouver + Vancouver Island sampler that builds to the wild Pacific coast. Two nights easing off jet-lag in Vancouver, a ferry to the Island for gardens + whales, then the big payoff — 3 nights in Tofino: surf beaches, old-growth rainforest, bears & whales. Different universe from a Med pool villa: think beach + rainforest + wildlife (pools are rare on the Island — hot tubs are the norm).
⚠️ Book now or lose it: at ~4 days out in peak July, Tofino lodges and BC Ferries vehicle reservations (Tsawwassen↔Swartz Bay) sell out — reserve the car on the ferry the moment dates are set. Also honest: this is a ~2× budget vs the Med options (long-haul + higher BC costs) and one day is a travel day.
Ballpark all-in (family of 3):
✈️ Flights ~€3,400 · 🚗 SUV 8d ~€800 · ⛴️ ferries ~€120 · 🏡 stays 8 nts ~€2,300 · ⛽/food/activities ~€1,200
