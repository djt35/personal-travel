Why this shape

Flying in/out of Edinburgh turns your remembered “up the west, down the east” route into a clean anticlockwise loop that starts & ends at the airport — dropping the old Lake District leg but cutting all the long driving. Five 2-night bases, no single-nighters, longest drive ~2h45 — built for a 5-year-old. Early June is deliberate: before Scottish school holidays (~24 Jun) and before midges peak.

Before you go — do these first

①Apply for 3× UK ETA at gov.uk/eta — £20 each (£60), Noah included. Valid 2 years, so fine to do any time before travel; needed to board.
②Book the Jacobite steam train the moment 2027 dates open (usually released late 2026) — it sells out months ahead. This is the one thing to grab early.
③Book flights BRU/AMS → Edinburgh when June-2027 schedules load (~autumn 2026). Direct from Brussels (Brussels Airlines, ~1h40) or Amsterdam (KLM/easyJet, more choice).
④Book a hire car — petrol/diesel estate or small SUV (skip EV hire = no Highland charging planning) + a booster seat for Noah, or bring your own.
⑤Book rooms ~6–9 months out — Fort William/Glencoe & Inverness fill early even in June. Pick one property per base below.
