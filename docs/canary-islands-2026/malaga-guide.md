# Villa Azafran (Almogía): restaurants, day trips and things to do, Sun 1 – Sat 7 Nov 2026

Research subagent, 30 Oct 2026, from web searches. Items marked *(unverified)* need a phone call or a check of the official site.

**Base:** Fuente Amarga countryside, between Almogía and Villanueva de la Concepción, about 450 m up. Villanueva sits at the foot of El Torcal, so the Torcal and Antequera are close. Málaga is about 45 min away and the coast about 55 min.

**Holiday check:** Sun 1 Nov is All Saints (Todos los Santos). Andalucía may move the public holiday to **Mon 2 Nov** *(unverified)*. That changes Monday closures and opening hours, so check with the host.

**Weather fit:** Sun 1 and Mon 2 are sunny at 20–22 °C. Tue 3 to Thu 5 are likely wet, with a possible downpour on Wed 4. The pool is unheated (about 16–19 °C).

## Day trips, ranked for Noah (5)

1. **El Torcal de Antequera**, about 25–35 min.
   - The Ruta Verde is 1.5 km and takes about 1 h. Karst rock sculptures and ibex; guides call it "perfect for children".
   - The visitor centre is open 10:00–17:00 in winter. Free, no booking.
   - The area is very sensitive to fog and rain at 1,200 m. **Go on Sun 1 or Mon 2, in the morning.**
2. **Antequera**, about 30–40 min.
   - UNESCO dolmens (Menga, Viera, El Romeral): free for EU citizens and partly under cover, so good on a showery day. **Closed Mondays.** Autumn hours are about Tue–Sat 09:00–18:00 and Sun 09:00–15:00 *(phone 952 71 22 06)*.
   - Alcazaba hill castle: a short walk.
   - Best day: Tue 3 (morning).
3. **Málaga city**, about 45 min. The main wet-day option.
   - MIMMA hands-on music museum *(hours unverified)*.
   - Museo Alborania – Aula del Mar, a small aquarium at Muelle Uno.
   - Centre Pompidou "Cubo", with a kids' area; usually closed Tuesdays.
   - Museo Picasso: about €13.50 per adult, with timed online tickets. Keep it short.
   - Alcazaba + Gibralfaro: **free on Sundays from 14:00**. Slippery when wet.
   - Mercado de Atarazanas: closed Sundays; open until about 15:00.
   - Muelle Uno and La Malagueta beach on a dry spell.
4. **Teleférico Benalmádena + Selwo Marina**, about 55 min.
   - Cable car: about 11:00–17:00 in Nov; stops in wind *(phone 952 577 773)*.
   - Selwo Marina (dolphins, penguins): mostly said to be open all year, 10:00–18:00, but **check selwomarina.es** for winter closures.
   - Bioparc Fuengirola is a strong alternative *(unverified)*.
5. **Nerja caves**, about 1 h 15. Winter hours (from 1 Nov) about 09:30–15:30. Kids 6–12 about €13; Noah, at 5, may pay. Indoors with many steps. Rain-day option.
6. **El Chorro / Caminito del Rey**: **not allowed for Noah.** The official rules say no children under 8, and age is checked. Instead, see the gorge from the El Chorro station bridge and the Ardales reservoir viewpoints, on dry days only.
7. **Ronda**, about 1 h 30. Spectacular, but too long a trip with a 5-year-old this week.
8. **Fuente de Piedra flamingos**: few birds and often distant in November. Only a detour from Antequera.
9. **Farm, donkey or horse experiences**: none could be verified. Ask the hosts (Charlotte & Charlton) about local riding stables or goat farms.

**Antequera**
- **Arte de Cozina**, C/ Calzada 27–29, phone 952 84 00 14.
  - Family-run, in a 17th-century house. Famous for three versions of **porra antequerana** (a thick cold tomato soup).
  - Finish with **bienmesabe** (an almond-sponge dessert).
  - The tapas bar is easy with kids.
- **Marmitia (Parador de Antequera)**: regional menu, spacious, earlier dinner (from about 20:00).
- **Mesón Juan Manuel**: highly rated porra.
- Breakfast **molletes** (soft bread rolls) on Calle Infante Don Fernando.

**Málaga city** *(details unverified)*
- **El Pimpi**: an iconic bodega below the Alcazaba. Order fried aubergine with honey. Food all day, so you can eat at 19:00.
- **El Tintero** (El Palo): waiters walk round shouting the names of dishes; kids love it. Order boquerones (fried anchovies) and espetos (sardine skewers).
- **Pedregalejo chiringuitos** (beach bars, e.g. Maricuchi): espetos.
- **Casa Lola**: tapas.
- **Atarazanas market stalls**: fried-fish lunch.

**Meal timing:** Málaga city serves food all day. Rural places and Antequera rarely serve dinner before 20:30. Make lunch the main meal and have a light dinner at the villa.

**Groceries:**
- Villanueva has small shops and a bakery.
- Do the big shop at **Mercadona or Lidl in Antequera** (about 30 min) or on the Málaga/Campanillas side.
- Many shops close on Sundays and holidays, so buy basics on the drive from the airport on Sun 1.

## Day-by-day (fits the weather)
- **Sun 1 (sunny):**
  - Land 12:45, collect the Hertz car, lunch at a venta on the A-357, buy basics.
  - Villa check-in from 15:00 (lockbox). A quick pool dip, if brave.
  - Dinner: Venta Hermanos Moreno or Mesón Jiménez.
- **Mon 2 (sunny, best day):**
  - El Torcal Ruta Verde at 10:00, then lunch at Arte de Cozina in Antequera.
  - The dolmens are likely closed (Monday), unless it's a holiday.
  - If El Torcal is in cloud: the Benalmádena cable car and Selwo Marina instead.
- **Tue 3 (showers likely, morning best):**
  - Antequera dolmens at 09:00, the Alcazaba if dry, big supermarket shop.
  - Lunch at the Parador or Mesón Juan Manuel.
- **Wed 4 (possible downpour):**
  - Málaga indoors: MIMMA, then Aula del Mar or the Pompidou.
  - Lunch at Atarazanas; early dinner at El Pimpi (19:00).
  - **Check AEMET warnings first**, and drive the villa track slowly after rain.
- **Thu 5 (wet):**
  - The Nerja caves, or Bioparc Fuengirola if it's only showery.
  - Lunch at El Tintero if there's a dry window.
- **Fri 6 (mixed/clearing):** Málaga waterfront (Muelle Uno, La Malagueta beach, playgrounds), or El Torcal again if it's clear. Last dinner at a venta.
- **Sat 7 (departure):** leave the villa by about 07:45 for VY2150 at 10:00. Allow about 50 min to the airport plus the car return.

## Practical
- **Roads:** the A-7075 / A-356 are winding mountain roads, and the last stretch to Fuente Amarga is a countryside track. Do it in daylight on Sun 1. After heavy rain, expect mud and water in dips; don't cross a flooded stream bed. Hire-car cover often excludes unpaved roads.
- **Fuel:** fill up in Málaga/Campanillas or Antequera (24 h stations by the A-45/A-92). The village stations have short Sunday hours.
- **Child seat:** under 135 cm needs an approved restraint, so Noah needs a **high-back booster** (add it to the Hertz booking).
- **Health:**
  - Villanueva has a health centre and a pharmacy.
  - Emergency departments: Hospital de Antequera, or the Málaga Regional/Clínico (about 45 min).
  - Emergencies: **112**. The duty pharmacy (farmacia de guardia) is posted on pharmacy doors.
- **Pool:** unheated, no fence or gate. Supervise Noah at all times.
