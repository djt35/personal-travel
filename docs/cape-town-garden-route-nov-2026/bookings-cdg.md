## 2. Recommendation: Park & Valet, covered (option A)

**Why:**
- **Kerbside drop-off and pickup at T2E**, so no shuttle and no walking with Noah and bags after an 11-hour day flight.
- The car is **charged to full** for the ~300 km night drive home.
- Covered parking, rated 9.2 on Parkos (827 reviews).
- Half the price of the official PCD E-Park, the only official car park that charges.

**The trade-off:** if David would rather keep the keys and never deal with a valet, choose **E (PCD E-Park, €272.30)**. It has guaranteed charging, is open 24/7, and is a 12-minute walk.

## 3. Drive plan

### Out: Sat 31 Oct, Drongen → CDG T2E (~300 km, ~3 h without traffic)
- **Bison Futé for Sat 31 Oct (bison-fute.gouv.fr, updated 28 Oct):**
  - departure direction green across France;
  - the **return direction is orange in Île-de-France**, "dense toute l'après-midi";
  - the official advice is to *reach or cross Île-de-France before 13:00 or after 20:00*.
  - CDG is on the northern edge of Île-de-France, but the A1 southbound from Senlis is in the return direction.
  - It is also the end of the Belgian (French-speaking) school holiday, plus ordinary Saturday shopping traffic. Fri 30 Oct is much worse, so don't go a day early.
- **Leave Drongen by 10:30** (11:00 at the latest), with the car at 100%. The route is:
  1. E17 to Kortrijk;
  2. A22 to Lille;
  3. A1 south.
- **Optional stop, Tesla Supercharger Roye:**
  - 110 rue Émile Pluchet, 80700 Roye, A1 exit 12;
  - 32 stalls, up to 250 kW, 24/7;
  - ~200 km in, ~100 km before CDG, around 12:30.
  - Take 15–20 min for a toilet break and a top-up. You don't strictly need it from 100%, but it's cheap insurance in Saturday traffic and costs nothing in time against the plan.
- **At T2E around 14:00–14:30,** ahead of the busiest Île-de-France afternoon traffic.
  - Phone Park & Valet when you are 30 min out (about Senlis or Survilliers).
  - The booked slot is 15:00, which leaves ~45–60 min of traffic buffer. Check-in for a long-haul flight closes about 60 min before departure.
- On the morning, check Waze or Google for the Lille ring (A1/A22 junction) and the A1 around Senlis.

### Return: Sun 8 Nov, CDG T2E → Drongen (landing 19:20, home ~23:30)
- **Bison Futé for Sun 8 Nov: green** (normal traffic).
- Phone Park & Valet on landing. Passport control plus bags at 2E usually takes ~45 min, so expect the car at the kerb around **20:15–20:30**, charged.
- Drive home in ~3 h through Lille and Kortrijk, arriving around 23:30.
- **Fallback if the car is not charged:**
  - **Tesla Supercharger Roissy-en-France, Hyatt Place:** 241 rue de la Belle Étoile, 12 stalls at 250 kW, 24/7, ~5 min from the terminals. A second Roissy Tesla site is at the Van der Valk hotel, 351 avenue du Bois de la Pie (12 stalls).
  - **Fastned at aire de Vémars on the A1:** CCS, 300 kW, just north of CDG on the way home.
  - **Roye Supercharger,** 24/7, about an hour into the drive.
  - Late-night Tesla Supercharger price is about €0.22/kWh.
