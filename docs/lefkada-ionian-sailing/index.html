Before you book

Licence: Greece doesn't accept RYA Day Skipper alone (since 2019). Bring the original ICC (ICC was ordered for the June plan — confirm the card is in hand). Lobke signs a “competent crew” declaration; bring all passports for the crew list.
🐈 Catamaran checkout: David has only ever skippered monohulls — never a cat. Book a checkout sail / handover briefing with the base (twin-throttle close-quarters handling, no keel = different feel) before slipping lines.
📅 Custody: confirm Noah is with David for the full Sat 5–Sat 12 Sep window (the June arrangement was date-specific — re-confirm for September).

⚓ Skipper's notes

Wind (Sept): NW maïstros sea-breeze, typically lighter than mid-summer — calm at dawn, builds to F3–4 by mid-afternoon, dies at dusk. Sail mornings, anchor by ~14:00, swim all afternoon. Watch for rare S winds (kills Fiskardo & Kastos) and the odd early-autumn thundery breakdown — check the forecast each morning.
Fuel: leave Lefkas full — no diesel at Kioni / Fiskardo / Kastos / Kalamos. Refuel only at the Lefkas marina dock (also Nidri/Vlicho).
Water: top up tanks at Spilia, Kioni (card), Fiskardo & Sivota. Carry full leaving Vathi.
Provisioning: big shop in Lefkada town first — Sklavenitis / AB / Lidl. Islands = bread, fruit, snacks only (Fiskardo pricey).
Forecast: Poseidon/HCMR + Windy / Windguru.
VHF: monitor Ch 16; call port police on Ch 12. Coastguard 108 (or 112).
Lefkas Canal bridge: floating swing bridge opens ≈ on the hour, one-way convoy — verify times with the marina on the day (2026 works schedule). Be back ~16:00 on hand-back day.
Paperwork & cash: confirm in writing that TEPAI tax is pre-paid. Carry the crew list (Belgian passports). Keep €100–150 cash for quay fees / water cards.
🛟 Child lifejacket: charter cats rarely carry child sizes — bring Noah's own (CE 50/100N, crotch strap + grab handle) and pre-request one in writing. Lifejacket on whenever he's on deck or in the dinghy.
Pre-request: SUP/kayak · extra fenders (6+) · autopilot · 30–50 m long line + shackle (for line-ashore bays) · full bimini.
