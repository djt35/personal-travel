# London City Trip: 17-20 March 2026

**Travellers:** David & Lobke (adults only)
**Hotel:** NoMad London, 28 Bow Street, Covent Garden, WC2E 7AW
**Nights:** 3 (Tue 17 - Fri 20 checkout)
**Vibe:** Packed, adult, shows + dinner every night

## Travel

**Eurostar** Brussels-Midi to London St Pancras — 2h direct
- Book via [eurostar.com](https://www.eurostar.com)
- St Pancras to NoMad: 20 min walk via King's Cross Road → Bloomsbury → Covent Garden, or 10 min taxi

## Day 1: Tuesday 17 March — Arrival + St Patrick's Day

### Morning/Afternoon — Travel + Explore
- Eurostar to London (aim for arrival ~12:00-13:00)
- Walk to NoMad via Holborn — soak up the city on foot
- **Check in at 15:00** (leave bags earlier if needed)
- **St Patrick's Day celebrations** in Trafalgar Square (free, festive atmosphere, 8 min walk from NoMad)
- Wander through Covent Garden Market piazza — right outside the hotel

### Pre-Show Drink — 17:30
**The Lamb and Flag** — oldest pub in Covent Garden (1623), 33 Rose Street
- 3 min walk from NoMad
- Classic pint in a Dickens-era pub before the show

### Dinner — 18:00
**Kiln** — 58 Brewer Street, Soho (10 min walk)
- Walk-in only, arrive 18:00 sharp for guaranteed counter seating
- Open-fire Thai cooking, smoky and intense
- ~£30-40pp — order the laap sausage and clay-pot curry
- Done by 19:00, stroll to theatre

### Show — 19:30
**DRACULA** — Cynthia Erivo (one-woman, 23 roles)
- Noel Coward Theatre, St Martin's Lane (5 min walk from Kiln)
- 1h 50m, no interval — intense, immersive solo performance
- Tickets from £30 — book via [delfontmackintosh.co.uk](https://www.delfontmackintosh.co.uk/whats-on/dracula)
- Out by ~21:20

### Post-Show — 21:30
**Opium** — 15-16 Gerrard Street, Chinatown (5 min walk)
- Hidden speakeasy behind Chinatown — three themed bars
- Apothecary cocktails, dim lighting, incredible atmosphere
- Walk back to NoMad (~8 min) when ready

---

## Day 2: Wednesday 18 March — Full Day + Relaxed 2-Star Dinner

### Morning Run — 06:30
**Thames South Bank 5K**
- NoMad → Waterloo Bridge (5 min walk) → cross to South Bank
- Run east past London Eye, Tate Modern, Shakespeare's Globe to Millennium Bridge
- Turn around → 5K total, flat, river views, virtually empty at 6:30am
- Back at NoMad by 07:15

### Breakfast — 07:30
NoMad hotel breakfast (excellent — the atrium restaurant is stunning in morning light)

### Morning — 09:30-12:30
**British Museum** — Great Russell Street (12 min walk from NoMad)
- Free entry, world-class collection
- Must-see: Rosetta Stone, Parthenon Marbles, Egyptian galleries
- 2-3 hours is enough for highlights

### Lunch — 12:30
**Speedboat Bar** — 58 Shaftesbury Avenue, Chinatown (8 min walk)
- Chaotic Bangkok street food, authentic Thai, loud and fun
- ~£20-30pp — one of the most enjoyable casual lunches in London

### Afternoon — 14:00-17:00
**Walk: Covent Garden → Westminster → St James's Park**
- Through Trafalgar Square (National Gallery if time — free)
- Down Whitehall past Downing Street, Horse Guards
- Westminster Abbey & Big Ben (exterior)
- St James's Park — early spring daffodils along the lake
- Back via The Mall past Buckingham Palace

### Pre-Dinner — 18:00
Back at NoMad to change. Drinks at the **NoMad Library Bar** (guests only — velvet, books, cocktails)
- No show tonight — dress up and take your time

### Dinner — 19:00
**Bonheur by Matt Abe** — 43 Upper Brook Street, Mayfair (10 min taxi)
- **2 Michelin stars** — opened in the legendary Le Gavroche space
- Journey menu £195pp / Dream menu £225pp
- Classical French, immaculate technique — THE meal of the trip
- Book via [bonheurbymattabe.com](https://www.bonheurbymattabe.com) or call 020 7139 8624 — **BOOK TODAY**
- No show to rush to — enjoy the full experience, linger over wine
- Allow 2.5-3 hours

### Post-Dinner — 22:00
**The Connaught Bar** — Carlos Place, Mayfair (5 min walk from Bonheur)
- World's #6 cocktail bar — legendary trolley martini service
- Perfect nightcap in Mayfair before taxi back to NoMad

## Day 3: Thursday 19 March — Double Show Day

### Morning Run — 06:30
**St James's Park → Hyde Park 5K**
- NoMad → Trafalgar Square → The Mall → St James's Park
- Loop the lake (Buckingham Palace views), through Green Park into Hyde Park
- 5K loop, flat, gorgeous dawn light on the lake
- Back at NoMad by 07:30

### Breakfast — 08:00
**The Wolseley** — 160 Piccadilly (15 min walk)
- Grand European cafe, one of London's great breakfast institutions
- Eggs Benedict, viennoiserie, excellent coffee — ~£25-30pp
- Walk-in usually fine at 8am on a Thursday

### Morning — 10:00-12:30
**Tower of London** — Tower Hill (20 min by Tube, Temple → Tower Hill)
- Crown Jewels, Yeoman Warder tours, ravens, medieval palace
- Book online to save ~20%: £34pp
- 2.5 hours is ideal

### Lunch — 13:00
**Borough Market** — Stoney Street, SE1 (10 min walk from Tower)
- Walk along the Thames from Tower Bridge to Borough Market
- Graze through the stalls: Padella (fresh pasta queue), Bread Ahead doughnuts, oysters, cheese
- ~£15-20pp grazing

### Show 1 (Matinee) — 14:30
**ROMEO & JULIET** — Sadie Sink & Noah Jupe, directed by Robert Icke
- Harold Pinter Theatre, 6 Panton Street (Tube: Borough → Piccadilly Circus, 15 min)
- 2h 40m including interval — out by ~17:10
- Tickets from £20 — book via [haroldpintertheatre.co.uk](https://www.haroldpintertheatre.co.uk/shows/romeo-and-juliet)

### Pre-Show Dinner — 17:30
**Osteria Vibrato** — 6 Greek Street, Soho (8 min walk from Harold Pinter)
- Charlie Mellor's theatrical Italian, 1 month old, 250-label wine list
- ~£60-80pp — pasta courses are exceptional
- Book via [SevenRooms](https://www.sevenrooms.com/explore/osteriavibrato)
- Done by 19:00, stroll to Duke of York's

### Show 2 (Evening) — 19:30
**TEETH 'N' SMILES** — Self Esteem (Rebecca Lucy Taylor) in David Hare's punk rock play
- Duke of York's Theatre, St Martin's Lane (5 min walk from NoMad)
- Dark, funny, explosive — rock star implodes at a Cambridge ball
- New original songs by Self Esteem + Hare's savage script
- Tickets ~£25-90 — book via [atgtickets.com](https://www.atgtickets.com) or [dukesofyorks.com](https://www.atgtickets.com/venues/duke-of-yorks-theatre/)

### Post-Show — ~21:30
**Christopher's Martini Bar** — 18 Wellington Street (3 min from NoMad)
- Art-deco cocktails to decompress after a packed day
- Or straight to NoMad Library Bar

---

## Day 4: Friday 20 March — Final Morning + Departure

### Morning Run — 07:00
**Regent's Canal 5K** (if departing from St Pancras)
- NoMad → King's Cross (20 min walk/jog) → Canal towpath north to Camden Lock → return
- 5K out-and-back, flat towpath, locks, narrowboats
- Finish at St Pancras area, ready for departure

### Breakfast — 08:30
**The Delaunay** — 55 Aldwych (8 min walk from NoMad)
- Grand Viennese cafe — kaiserschmarrn, full English, pastries
- ~£20-25pp — perfect last breakfast

### Late Morning — 10:00-12:00
**David Hockney exhibition** — Serpentine North Gallery, Hyde Park
- Opens 12 March, runs through August — free entry
- New paintings + the 90m "A Year in Normandy" frieze
- Combined with a stroll through Hyde Park / Kensington Gardens

### Checkout & Depart
- Back to NoMad, collect bags
- Walk or taxi to St Pancras for Eurostar home
- ~2h Brussels-Midi, then drive to Drongen
