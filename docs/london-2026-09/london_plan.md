**Around 16:00:** leave for King’s Cross. Allow 5–10 minutes to walk to **Stratford International National Rail**, then take Southeastern highspeed to **St Pancras**. The operator advertises a [seven-minute train journey](https://www.southeasternrailway.co.uk/trains/stratford-international-to-london-st-pancras); allow roughly 30–40 minutes hotel-to-King’s-Cross waterfront including waiting and walking. These are estimates; the date-specific service and engineering works have not been checked. [Oyster/contactless](https://www.southeasternrailway.co.uk/help/support/faqs/tickets/oyster-and-contactless) works on this particular highspeed link, with a separate fare outside daily/weekly caps. Check a child ticket for the five-year-old; do not assume Tube child rules apply.

The live three-person search on 9 September showed 17:00, but the confirmation panel said “Dining Table” after selecting drinks: the intended drinks booking type needs confirmation, as does child admission.

Availability may change; child admission remains to be confirmed before reserving.
